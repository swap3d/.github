# Code Of Conduct

## Our Standards

We are committed to a respectful, welcoming, and productive community.
Examples of positive behavior include:

- using inclusive and considerate language
- respecting differing viewpoints and experience
- giving and receiving constructive feedback
- focusing on what is best for users and the community
- showing empathy toward other participants

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks,
sexualized language or attention, intimidation, deliberate disruption, and
publishing another person's private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these
standards. They may edit, reject, or remove contributions and may temporarily
or permanently restrict participation when behavior is inappropriate,
threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Report conduct concerns privately to `support@swap3d.studio`. Reports will be
reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially as practical.

## Scope

This policy applies in Swap3D repositories and community spaces, and when an
individual is officially representing Swap3D in public spaces.

This policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1, available
at https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html.
